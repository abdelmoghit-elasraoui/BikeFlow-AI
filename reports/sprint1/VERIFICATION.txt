=====================================================================
7. KNOWN LIMITATIONS (state these honestly)
=====================================================================

- Training/test period is Dec-Feb (winter only); summer behaviour unseen.
- Weather and explicit holiday features are not implemented yet.
- Capacity and station lifecycle are approximated by the first/last observed trip day (docs/eligibility.md).
- 12-month expansion, PostgreSQL, and the Poisson baseline remain open for Sprint 2.
- The warning score is a transparent priority score, not a calibrated probability.
- The API is not publicly deployed; it is accessed through an SSH tunnel. Docker + CI/CD is Sprint 4 work per the teacher requirements.
